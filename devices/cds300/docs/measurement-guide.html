Bench measurements

Everything is on your own photos of the device: yellow circles are where to touch, red is where not to. Tap a photo to enlarge it. The fields are saved in this browser, and at the end a button copies everything for the chat.

Safety

  • 230 V on the power-supply board (the upright board on the left, with the transformer). With the device plugged in, keep your hands away from it.
  • −27 V and the display filament on the 4-pin W702 connector, at the left end of the front board (red area in the photos). Don't touch.
  • One hand only with the device on; the black probe held by a crocodile clip.
  • Any value above 3.4 V on the lines: stop and send it before going on. The page paints the field red.
  • Beep (continuity) only with the device unplugged.
